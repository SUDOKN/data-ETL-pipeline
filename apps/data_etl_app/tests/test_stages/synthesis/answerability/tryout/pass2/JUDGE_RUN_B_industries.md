# Run-B tryout — judge report, field: INDUSTRIES

Arms: **c16** (`c16_system.txt`, production) vs **b** (`b_industries_system.txt` = c16 + the section
"## What to settle about the dealing"). Requests: `ind_mw1` (mathewsco, 32 records) and `ind_sc4`
(steelcraft, 27 records). Five repeats per arm per request; every record judged as a MODE over its
five repeats. 590 paragraphs read.

---

## 1. Headline

**The added section hurts.** Across the 59 records, the mode is a defensible reading in **55/59 for
c16 (93%)** and **35/59 for b (59%)**. Every difference is on one subject: on mathewsco — the
manufacturers' sales representative, where the party question is the whole game — **20 of 32 records
flip from right (c16) to a party over-claim (b)**, and **0 flip the other way**. The mechanism is
uniform: where a snippet shows a *represented maker's* served market or application ("Hudson
Technologies' seamless enclosures … are integral to *irrigation* … applications"; "*we* serve major
customers in the *oil & gas* … industries" on Ellwood City Forge's hosted page), c16 keeps the doing
with the maker and gives Mathews the listing/representing part in 5/5 repeats, while b, in 3 of its 5
repeats, hands Mathews a supplying dealing into that sector — "*its own dealing is as a sales
representative supplying into the oil & gas sector*", "*thus supplies products into the irrigation
industry*", "*representing this supplier to customers in the mechanical seals sector*". That is the
laundering the calibration ruling names, in its softened form, and it is mode-level (3/5) on all 20
maker-side records; on one of them (`g3mz9c6m`, "open-die forging industry") it is plainly wrong-side:
b turns Ellwood's own *line of work* into a market Mathews supplies into.

**On the records the section was written for, it does nothing.** The two steelcraft targets —
`gotfeskc` "Healthcare institutions" and `gwlu4iz5` "institutions", which the production run
under-claimed — are under-claimed identically by both arms, 5/5 each: "*This is a general statement
about healthcare institutions and does not show any specific dealing by Steelcraft*", on Steelcraft's
own **APPLICATIONS ▸ HEALTHCARE** page whose very next line reads "Below are a number of special
features as part of Steelcraft's portfolio of products". The section's clause "nor into no dealing"
never beat c16's "Nobody's sentence" paragraph. The same happens on `g69h41m1` ("business of steel
doors and frames"), where the section's "the manufacturer's own line of work called by the same kind
of word" clause should have fired and did not (0/5 both arms). **All 27 sc4 records have identical
modes in the two arms: 23/27 right in both, 4/27 under-claimed in both.**

Length: b inflates where it fires (mw1 mean 60.0 → 65.8 words, +9.7%) and not where it doesn't
(sc4 52.9 → 53.1). b introduces a per-repeat formulaic closer that carries a claim
("; the capacity is as a sales representative.", "; its own dealing is as a sales representative
supplying into the <X> sector.") repeated verbatim across ~20 records inside one repeat.

One genuine, sub-mode gain for b: it drops c16's two habitual tics on the mathewsco maker records —
the wire word in "*but the snippet does not show Mathews & Company itself manufacturing…*" (c16[0],
~20 records) and the unasked denial "*but does not itself manufacture X*" (c16[2]). These are
repeat-level (1/5 each), not mode-level, and they do not offset 20 flipped records.

## 3. Defects introduced by b

1. **Party over-claim: a represented maker's market becomes the representative's own supplying
   dealing.** Mode-level (3/5) on all 20 Class-A records of ind_mw1.
   - `gmjhc9z1` b[2]: "Mathews & Company represents Hudson Technologies and, by extension, lists edge
     welded bellows as a destination market for the products of the companies it represents; **its own
     dealing is as a sales representative supplying into the edge welded bellows sector**."
   - `gu7i4rim` b[1]: "Mathews & Company represents Hudson Technologies and **thus supplies products
     into the semi-conductor industry** by representing manufacturers whose products are used in
     semi-conductor applications."
   - `gp3tnbmk` b[0]: "In all these cases, Mathews & Company is representing **or supplying into** the
     medical sector, either by representing suppliers whose products are used in medical applications
     **or by supplying products for medical OEMs**." (Here "medical" is also in Mathews' own Markets
     Covered, so the claim happens to land; on `geqf44x4` automotive and `glf8up19` electrical, which
     are **not** in Markets Covered, the identical construction has no support: `geqf44x4` b[2] "the
     company's own dealing is to act as a sales representative for these manufacturers, **supplying
     products into the automotive sector**.")
2. **Customers imported into a sector the text never puts them in.** Mode-level in b[0] across the 20
   Class-A records: `gavy5blu` b[0]: "the dealing is Mathews & Company representing this supplier **to
   customers in the flow meters sector**."
3. **Wrong side: a line of work read as a destination market.** `g3mz9c6m`, 3/5 — b[1]: "Mathews &
   Company represents Ellwood City Forge Group and thus **supplies products into the open-die forging
   industry** by representing manufacturers whose products are used in that sector." Ellwood *is* in
   the open-die forging industry; nothing is supplied into it.
4. **Formulaic closer carrying a claim.** Repeat-level but pervasive: b[4] ends ~20 consecutive mw1
   records with "; **the capacity is as a sales representative.**"; b[2] ends ~19 with "; its own
   dealing is as a sales representative supplying into the <X> sector." c16 has a matching tic
   ("the dealing is representation, not direct manufacture or supply by Mathews & Company", c16[3]),
   so this is a difference of content, not of habit: c16's template denies, b's template asserts.
5. **Length inflation where the section fires.** ind_mw1 mean 60.0 → 65.8 words (+9.7%); the longest
   b paragraphs run to 162w (`geqf44x4` b[4], `gp3tnbmk` b[3] 154w). No inflation on ind_sc4.

Not observed on these two requests: option-list echo, refusal of the manufacturer's own listings,
naming a page the text does not show (b's page names were accurate throughout; the one wobble,
`g9r82sq8` "H Series Flush Doors product page" vs the Windstorm page, occurs in **both** arms since
the sentence appears on both pages), dropping the focal entity, first-person leaks. The wire word
"snippet" leaks in both arms (c16 more: "but the snippet does not show…" in c16[0] on ~20 mw1
records; b uses it on ind_sc4 only, "This snippet describes…").

## 4. Wording

The section is three sentences. On my 59 records:

**Sentence 1 — the cause of every defect above.**
> "Where the focal entity is a field of activity, a market, or a kind of customer, say on which side of
> the manufacturer's dealings it stands: the people the manufacturer makes for or sells into, its
> product going into it, its supplying those who work in it, a customer from it coming to the
> manufacturer; or the manufacturer's own line of work called by the same kind of word; or a channel it
> sells through; or whatever else the snippets show."

Every option offered puts **the manufacturer inside the dealing**. There is no option for "this market
is another party's, and the manufacturer's part is only to list, carry, or represent that party" —
which is the true answer for 20 of 32 mathewsco records. Forced to name a side, the model picks the
nearest manufacturer-side option ("its supplying those who work in it") and launders the maker's market
into Mathews'. Smallest rewrite — add the missing side to the list:

> …or a channel it sells through; **or another party's market, which the manufacturer only lists,
> carries, or represents, the doing staying with that party;** or whatever else the snippets show.

**Sentence 2 — did not fire where it was needed; contributed where it was not.**
> "Serving, supplying into, or making for it is a dealing in its own right: say it as such, and do not
> turn it into making the focal entity itself, nor into no dealing."

"nor into no dealing" was written for `gotfeskc`/`gwlu4iz5` and lost 0/5 to c16's stronger "Nobody's
sentence" paragraph, which tells the model that "a sentence about what companies … in general do … is
not a statement about this manufacturer". The section cannot win that contest by implication; it has to
name the frame. Smallest rewrite that could reach it:

> Serving, supplying into, or making for it is a dealing in its own right: say it as such **and say
> whose it is**; do not turn it into making the focal entity itself, nor into no dealing. **A sentence
> saying what such customers need, under a heading that names that market on a page the manufacturer
> gives to its own offerings for it, shows the manufacturer making and offering for that market, not a
> sentence about no one.**

**Sentence 3 — never fired; unvalidated, not validated.**
> "Where the same word also names the manufacturer's own process, department, or equipment, the
> snippet's words decide which it is."

No record in either request produced a paragraph where this distinction was live — `gvsg9rsy` "machine
shop", `gv6n9uho` "machinery" and `gu5qlwyn` "mechanical" were the only candidates, and neither arm ever
read them as Mathews' own process. The sentence carries no evidence either way from this field.

## 5. Verdict

**DO NOT SHIP.** As drafted the section fixes nothing on the records it was written for (0/5 on both
steelcraft under-claim targets, and 0/5 on `g69h41m1`, with all 27 sc4 modes unchanged) while creating
a mode-level party over-claim on 20 of 32 mathewsco records — it hands a represented maker's served
market to the sales representative, which is the one error this subject exists to catch. A redraft must
add the "another party's market, which the manufacturer only lists or represents" side to sentence 1's
option list and make sentence 2 name the applications-page frame explicitly, and must then be
re-measured on both requests before shipping.
