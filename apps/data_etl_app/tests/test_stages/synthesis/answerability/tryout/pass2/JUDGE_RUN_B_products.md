# Run-B tryout — judge report, PRODUCTS

Arms: **c16** (`c16_system.txt`, production) vs **b** (`b_products_system.txt` = c16 + "## What to settle about
the dealing"). Requests: `prod_ag36` (agstech, 44 records) and `prod_al15` (alecmfg, 32 records), five repeats
per arm = **760 paragraphs**, all read. Delivery was complete in both arms: 44/44 and 32/32 records in every
one of the twenty responses, no duplicates, no extras.

The added section is three sentences. Naming them for the rest of this report:

- **S1** — the capacity list ("say in what capacity they fix it … a thing it makes and sells as its own; a
  thing it makes or works on to another party's order …").
- **S2** — "These are shapes such a dealing commonly takes, not a list to choose from: **where the snippets fix
  none of them, say the dealing as shown and that the capacity is unstated**, as with an entry on a line card
  or a list that says nothing about who made the things listed; **where the sentence introducing a list fixes
  it, the item takes it**, as said above."
- **S3** — "Where the snippets present the focal entity as something other than a thing dealt in, a kind of
  work, a substance, a document, a heading, say what they present it as."

## 3. Defects introduced by b

1. **Capacity-unstated hardening on a shop's own material table — mode-level, 24 records.**
   c16 carries the tail in 71 of 120 material paragraphs (3/5 per record); b in **118 of 120** (5/5 on every
   record). Verbatim, `g50ywm4y` b[2]: *"This shows that Alec Model offers A380 as a material option for its
   manufacturing services, **with the capacity unstated**."* And `gv8l15jj` b[1]: *"…with the specific capacity
   unstated."* The mode was already hedged in c16, so this is not a new mode — but b removes the 2/5 of
   repeats that got it right and makes the under-claim unanimous, which is what a production A/B would see as
   J2 minors rising across a whole field.

2. **Mode flip on `g7tqyrnr` (wafer handling system) — mode-level, 1 record.** b's majority drops the
   supplying-into finding. b[3]: *"The snippets show no dealing by Alec Model in wafer handling systems
   themselves."* b[0]: *"they do not show Alec Model dealing in wafer handling systems."* Only b[1] and b[2]
   keep it ("the destination for components supplied in the project"). c16 keeps it 3/5.

3. **A page and section the text does not show — single repeat, `gdzg72p1` b[0].** Verbatim: *"'CNC prototype
   manufacturing' appears as a phrase on Alec Model's **homepage, under the section 'We Do it Differently,'**
   where the company describes its approach to precision and quality in prototype manufacturing."* The phrase
   occurs exactly once in the text, on `https://alecmfg.com/about-us/`, after the "2022: Securing a Global
   Leadership Position" history block. "We Do it Differently" is a homepage heading whose text is "Precision
   Quality Prototype Manufacturing" — a different phrase. All five c16 repeats place it correctly on the about
   page. This is the only fabricated placement in the 760 paragraphs, and it is b's.

4. **Sharpened denial of the thing in a case-study title — 2/5 on five records** (`grd8rfp0`, `gtm5awem`,
   `gq0675iq`, `gcamt5fn`, `gn3k6oqw`), 0/5 in c16. b[3]/b[4] close with *"…offering this case study as a
   resource, **not the structural housing itself**."* c16 stops at "do not show any further dealing with the

## 4. Wording

- **S2, first half — "where the snippets fix none of them, say the dealing as shown and that the capacity is
  unstated" — is the cause of defect 1.** On a page whose own heading reads "Material Options — A variety of
  materials are available for the manufacturing processes we offer", the frame *does* fix the dealing (Alec
  works these materials in the processes it offers), but the sentence reads as a standing instruction to
  append an unstated-capacity clause whenever no explicit maker/reseller word is present. Smallest rewrite —
  add the material and own-page carve-out the calibration rulings already contain:
  > *"…where the snippets fix none of them, say the dealing as shown and that the capacity is unstated, as
  > with an entry on a line card or a list that says nothing about who made the things listed. **A material,
  > grade, or alloy listed on the manufacturer's own page of what it works in is one it works in: say that,
  > and do not ask whether it also makes or buys the material.**"*
- **S2, second half — "where the sentence introducing a list fixes it, the item takes it" — fired correctly
  and is the reason agstech did not regress** (26 records, 130 paragraphs, zero hedges). It duplicates the
  list rule already in c16, so it validates c16's rule, not the new section.
- **S1 — the capacity list — never fired on any of these 76 records.** Not one b paragraph names a capacity
  that the matching c16 paragraph left out; the shapes it enumerates ("makes and sells as its own", "works on
  to another party's order", "resells, distributes, or represents") appear nowhere in b's prose. On this field
  it is **unvalidated, not validated** — and note that `prod_al15` contains the clearest "works to another
  party's order" case in the pair (the 30 valve bodies machined for the semiconductor client) and S1 did not
  fire there either.
- **S3 — "a kind of work, a substance, a document, a heading, say what they present it as"** — fired twice,
  once well (`gdzg72p1`, firmer own-capability reading) and once badly (defect 4: it pushes the paragraph from
  "the snippets show no further dealing" to the active denial "not the valve body itself", on titles where the
  ruling says the title names the work). If the section is kept, add to S3:
  > *"…say what they present it as — **but a case-study or project title that names work done on a thing still
  > names that work: do not deny the work to say the entry is a document.**"*

---

## 5. Verdict

**DO NOT SHIP** the products section as drafted: its one validated sentence duplicates a rule c16 already has,
its capacity list never fired on 76 records, and the only measurable effects on this field are negative — a
unanimous capacity-unstated under-claim across a shop's own 24-grade material table (71 → 118 of 120
paragraphs), one mode flip that loses a supplying-into finding, and the run's only fabricated page placement.
If it is kept for cross-field consistency, ship it only with both edits in §4 (the material carve-out in S2 and
the case-study clause in S3), and re-measure.
