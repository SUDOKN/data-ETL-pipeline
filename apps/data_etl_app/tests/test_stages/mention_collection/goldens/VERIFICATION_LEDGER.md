# Golden-corpus verification ledger

Promotion policy (`GOLDEN_LABELS_SCHEMA.md`): labels are seeded as `candidate`;
a **second independent reader** promotes to `confirmed`; only `confirmed` gates.
`disputed` never gates. The user may veto anything.

Run `.venv/bin/python checks/validate_goldens.py --quotes` for live numbers — it
re-derives every count from the files, so this ledger cannot drift away from the
corpus it documents.

## Round 1 — the corpus seed (2026-08-27)

`acceptable_forms` are precisely the strings this stage is handed, so grading
"given the right forms, does the stage handle them correctly" needed no
invention. Occurrence labels were generated by
`checks/seed_goldens.py`, which calls the production `collect_window` — the
corpus is therefore an exact oracle for the mechanical half rather than a
second opinion about it.

harvesting the three sibling instruments' profiles first and then reading the

**Shape notes.** `agstech_net` is 46% of the corpus by label count (6,721 of
14,713) because it names ~110 processes and ~44 materials in one document; any
corpus-wide rate is effectively an agstech rate unless it is computed per
subject. `taylordunn_com` has 14 labels and 5 empty fields because its scrape is

probe, and its value is that almost everything found on it should be nothing.
`austinelectricservices_com` and `med-tekinc_com` are the other deliberate
near-empties.

## Corrections this round made to prior documents

Each was found by measuring, and each is recorded where it will be read again
(`SEEDING_BRIEF.md` §6, `GOLDEN_LABELS_SCHEMA.md`).

1. **`Hemming` / "HEM Saw" was two forms conflated.** `Hemming` on blackadvtech
   has zero hits of any kind — a pure fabrication. The 51 false positives belong
   to `HEM`, which also genuinely occurs three times as the band-saw brand.
   Opposite labels.
2. **The historical "`Lead`, 52 hits, 0 real" does not reproduce.** `Lead` is a
   real whole word on 16 of 20 subjects. Its defect is sense, not occurrence.
3. **This schema's own worked example was invented** — it claimed 14 occurrences
   of a form that has 120, and did not mention that 54 of them are one repeated
   footer line.
4. **taylordunn** is 31 of 31 identical pages, not 30 of 31; boilerplate is
   99.3% of non-blank lines, not ~97.5%.
5. **superiortech's equipment census is 152 machine units**, not ~100.
6. **Country dropdowns**: sterlingmfg 249 entries, lucasmilhaupt 194 — both
   previously recorded as "~240". decimal's really is 248.
7. **sterlingmfg's invisible characters are line-trailing**, not mid-phrase.
8. **med-tekinc has 12 non-breaking spaces, not 10**, and one of them sits
   inside the subject's core capability (`HEAT<NBSP>TREATING`), contradicting
   the claim that none split a form.
9. **A search hazard quote does not byte-match its own file** — mathewsco's
   `Plastic Injection and Insert Molding` uses non-breaking spaces throughout;
   only an elastic matcher finds it.

## Raised, deliberately not labelled

**`TIG` on howcogroup is a matcher artifact, not a fact about the text.** The
site writes "TiG"; forms of three characters or fewer are matched
case-sensitively by both `_shared/text_matching` and the pipeline's own
`floor_scan`, so it scores zero. Labelling it `must_not_occur` would enshrine
the artifact as ground truth. Recorded in `../BOUNDARY.md` for the owner of the
shared matcher.
