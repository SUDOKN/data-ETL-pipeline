You are given text scraped from a manufacturer's website, and a list of mentions provided at the very bottom: first the mention ids, fenced between <<<MENTION_IDS and MENTION_IDS>>>, then the mentions themselves, fenced between <<<MENTIONS and MENTIONS>>>. Each mention holds its mention_id and a passage copied verbatim from the text — a sentence, or a whole line where the passage is not a sentence. The text holds the site's pages one after another, each page beginning with its address on a line of its own.

## Task
For each mention, find the passage in the text and describe, in your own words, where it sits, with sufficient detail in order to make sense of the passage. It may be what the page is about, what section or heading the passage falls under, and what kind of text it is — a heading, a navigation or menu item, an entry in a list, a table cell, a sentence of prose, a quotation or testimonial from someone, a news or blog item, a case-study listing, a job posting, a footer, boilerplate that repeats across pages, or something else. Where the text shows who is speaking, say so: the site's own copy, a customer, a publication, another party. Where the text does not show where a passage sits — say what the text shows and no more, rather than guessing.

Describe location only. Do not judge, summarize, or restate what the passage says.

Name a page by what it is about, in your own words — never by copying its address, or any part of it, into a description.

Each description must stand on its own. Never refer to another mention or to an earlier description — not "the previous mention", "the same table as above", "another item in this list" — name the place itself each time, even when that repeats what you wrote for a neighbour.

A passage may occur at more than one place in the text — the same menu item on every page, the same boilerplate line. Describe it once, covering where it recurs.

## Output
Return a JSON object with a single key "mentions" whose value is an array with one object per given mention — every given mention_id exactly once, copied exactly as given, and no others — each holding the mention_id and its location.

```json
{"mentions": [
  {"mention_id": "<a given mention_id>",
   "location": "<where this passage sits, in your own words>"},
  {"mention_id": "<another given mention_id>",
   "location": "<where that passage sits>"}
]}
```
