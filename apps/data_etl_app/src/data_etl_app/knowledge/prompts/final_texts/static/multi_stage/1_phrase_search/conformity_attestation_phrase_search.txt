You are analyzing text scraped from a manufacturer's website. The text is provided at the very bottom. It holds the site's pages one after another, each preceded by that page's URL. The URL lines count as text too: the wording in a path often works like a heading for its page.

## Task
Your task is to find every phrase in the text that names, states or implies a certification, accreditation, registration, standard, or compliance. Every phrase you return must occur in the text word for word, as one contiguous span of whole words. Where the text allows, extract separate phrases for separate certifications, accreditations, registrations, standards, or compliances rather than one phrase that bundles several together.

## What qualifies as a phrase
A qualifying phrase names, states or implies a certification, accreditation, registration, standard, or compliance — one with an identity of its own, such as a name, number, mark, or issuing body, that a company, its people, its sites, or its products could specifically hold, meet, or follow.

## Rejection
Do not extract a phrase if it does not name, state or imply any certification, accreditation, registration, standard, or compliance at all, even with the help of surrounding context.

## Extent
Try to keep each phrase short by focusing on and extracting the designation, not the statement made about it. Rather than extracting the long span as a single phrase, split it into shorter verbatim sub-phrases — but keep only the qualifying sub-phrases that each still name, state or imply a certification, accreditation, registration, standard, or compliance on their own.

## Deduplication/Disambiguation/Uniqueness
Phrases that are merely different wordings of each other — through a different casing, spacing or hyphenation, punctuation, '&' versus 'and', spelling, inflection (singular versus plural, or verbal versus nominal forms of the same word), or otherwise something similarly trivial — are not considered distinct enough to be recorded separately.

## Output
Return a JSON object with a single key "phrases" whose value is an array of strings — one per distinct phrase, each copied verbatim from the text. Preserve original wording — do not paraphrase or normalize. If no phrases are found, use an empty array.

```json
{"phrases": ["phrase one", "phrase two", ...]}
```
