You are given the name of a manufacturer and a list of records holding entries collected from text scraped from its website. You do not see the website itself: each entry was written down by a reader who did, and the entries are all you have. The records are provided at the very bottom: first the record ids, fenced between <<<RECORD_IDS and RECORD_IDS>>>, then the records themselves, fenced between <<<RECORDS and RECORDS>>>. Each record holds its record_id, its focal_form — the name of the one entity the record is about, spelled as it appears on the site — and its entries; each entry holds a snippet — a passage copied verbatim from the site in which the focal form appears — and, where given, the location the reader found it at in the original text.

## Task
Each record names a focal entity (its focal_form) and holds the entries a reader collected for it. Work each record on its own and write one synthesis for it: a single, well-put-together statement that describes the focal entity using the record's entries as evidence — what the entries show the manufacturer does, has, or is with respect to that entity, and what is said of it — in the entries' own attributions, with the locations, where given, woven in where they tell the reader something (a menu item, a heading, a case study, a customer's words). Concentrate on the focal entity: an entry that uses the focal form in some other sense, or says nothing about the entity, is not evidence for the record — leave it aside rather than weaving it in.

## Rules on reporting
Report what the entries say about the focal entity and add nothing of your own. Assert nothing the entries do not support, and attribute every statement to whoever the entries attribute it to: the site's own copy, a named or unnamed customer, a publication, another party. Where an entry places something outside the present — a completed project, a plan, a denial — carry that placement in the entry's own words.
Where entries repeat the same thing — the same menu item on every page, the same boilerplate line — say it once and note that it recurs.
Do not emit the manufacturer's name anywhere in your output. Where it would appear inside a quotation, put '[the manufacturer]' in its place — the brackets stay visible, so the edit shows. Treat obvious derivatives of the name the same way: the name without legal endings, its initials, its web domain. Other companies' names are quoted as written and attributed as the entries attribute them.
Before you return your answer, read back every string you wrote and check that no mention of the manufacturer's name survived anywhere in it.

## Output
Return a JSON object with a single key "syntheses" whose value is an array with one object per given record — every given record_id exactly once, copied exactly as given, and no others — each holding the record_id and its synthesis.

```json
{"syntheses": [
  {"record_id": "<a given record_id>",
   "synthesis": "<what the entries say about the focal entity — what they show the manufacturer does, has, or is with respect to it, and what is said of it — in the entries' own attributions, with their locations where given>"},
  {"record_id": "<another given record_id>",
   "synthesis": "<...>"}
]}
```
