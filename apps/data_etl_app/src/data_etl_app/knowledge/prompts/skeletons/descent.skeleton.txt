You will be given a list of records, each carrying a masked id. Each record gathers the evidence about one recurring subject in text scraped from a manufacturer's website: a "focal_form" — the wording the site itself uses for that subject — and a "synthesis" of what the text says about it and what the manufacturer does with or in relation to it. You will also be given the narrower kinds of {{parent_entity}} the vocabulary holds, written as an outline. Identification is your whole job here; relationships are not. Whose dealing it is plays no part. What you owe each narrower kind is the record's own words: general evidence of {{parent_entity}} proves no narrower kind of it.

### How to answer:
